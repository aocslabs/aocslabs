## How we work

- **Local-first.** Your code, keys and data stay on your machine. No hidden uploads, no telemetry.
- **One job per tool.** Small binaries and small extensions that are easy to read and easy to replace.
- **Licensed for reuse.** MIT for source; third-party assets keep their own licences and are listed in each repo.
- **Security work only with authorisation.** Tooling here is for your own systems, labs and signed engagements.
